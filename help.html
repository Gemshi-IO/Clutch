Getting Started

What You Need

  • Apple Device: iPhone or iPad running a recent iOS / iPadOS release (iOS 26+ recommended for on-device FoundationModels).
  • iCloud (Optional but Recommended): Signed into your Apple account to enable automatic cross-device sync across iPhone and iPad without any third-party accounts.
  • Apple Intelligence (Optional): Enabled on supported devices for auto-generated taxonomy tags, Extract Core Info (recipes, tutorials, takeaways), Suggest a Shelf, and natural language search.

First Launch Onboarding

When you launch Clutch for the first time, you’ll be guided through a lightweight 5-step introduction:

  1. Welcome: The principles of zero guilt, zero tracking, and on-device speed.
  2. Share Sheet Integration: How to save links and videos in one tap from Safari, YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram.
  3. Library Overview: Introducing non-destructive shelves and automatic media type filtering.
  4. Daily Digest: An audio overview of how unread articles transform into a morning spoken podcast.
  5. Get Started: Enter your inbox immediately.
💡
You can skip onboarding at any time. Clutch is instantly ready to capture and organize bookmarks.
02

Saving Links

Clutch is built around a non-negotiable principle: Never lose a save. If you’re offline, on airplane mode, or the destination server fails to parse, the URL lands safely in your local inbox immediately.

Save from Another App (Fastest)

  1. Open the article, video, or social post in Safari, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, or Reddit.
  2. Tap the system Share button.
  3. Select Clutch from the Share Sheet.
  4. Confirm — you’ll see a subtle checkmark confirmation in under a second.
🧭
Safari Tip: Sharing directly from Safari sends rich DOM metadata, giving you clean titles and high-res thumbnails immediately. When sharing from social apps, Clutch captures the raw URL and completes the enrichment the moment you open the app.

Save Inside the App

  1. On the Home screen, tap the floating + button.
  2. Select Add Link.
  3. Paste or type any web link (optionally provide a custom title).
  4. Optionally assign tags or a collection shelf right away.
  5. Tap Save.

Gentle Clipboard Offer

When you return to Clutch with a valid web link on your iOS clipboard, you may see an unobtrusive “Link on clipboard?” banner. Tapping it opens the pre-filled Add Link modal. Clutch never silently monitors your clipboard in the background.

What Happens After You Save

Bookmarks appear in your inbox immediately, then enrich locally in the background:

  • Cleanses marketing trackers (UTM parameters, referral tags) from titles and URLs.
  • Extracts distraction-free offline article text.
  • Generates content-derived color tints and caches thumbnails.
  • Tags the content automatically using on-device Apple Intelligence.

A soft shimmer indicates an item is still enriching. If a connection drops, a Tap to retry prompt appears.

Zero Duplicate Clutter

Re-saving an existing URL simply refreshes its position to the top of your inbox and informs you it was already saved. Your library never clutters with duplicate records.

03

Saving Notes

Notes in Clutch are first-class citizen items — not just annotations attached to links.

Create a Standalone Note

  1. On Home, tap + and select Add Note.
  2. Type your thoughts, quick thoughts, or checklists.
  3. Attach up to 5 photos directly from your camera roll.
  4. Apply Bold and Italic formatting inline.
  5. Tap Done to save.

Share Plain Text as a Note

Highlight text in Apple Notes or any other app and share to Clutch. If the text contains a URL, Clutch intelligently treats it as a bookmark; otherwise, it creates a rich local Note.

Viewing & Checklists

Tap any note card to enter reader mode. Checklist items (formatted with brackets or bullet lines) can be checked off interactively right in the reader view.

04

Your Inbox (Home)

The Home view is your living reading pile — designed to feel welcoming, never overwhelming.

Flexible Layout Modes

  • Masonry Grid (Default): Responsive card sizes derived from real content aspect ratios (vertical TikToks, square IG photos, horizontal article cards).
  • Compact List: Dense linear rows displaying thumbnail, platform badge, and clean title for high-speed scanning.

Switch layouts instantly using the grid/list toggle in the top action bar.

Card Gestures

Gesture Action Description
Tap Open Opens distraction-free reader or launches host media player.
Swipe Right Archive Marks item as read and moves it gracefully into the Archive tray.
Swipe Left Organize Opens sheet to quickly toggle tags and collection shelves.
Long Press Context Menu Access full suite of actions (Pin, Remind, Share, Select, Delete).

14-Day Gentle Aging

Bookmarks older than 14 days subtly reduce opacity and group into an Aging sub-section. A dismissible Tidy Up affordance allows you to batch-archive the entire aging pile in a single tap with full undo support.

05

Reading & Watching

Distraction-Free Reader

Tap any text-based article or Reddit post to open Clutch’s custom reading surface:

  • Theme Controls: Choose between System, Warm Sepia, Crisp Paper, and OLED Black.
  • Typography & Spacing: Adjust font sizing, line height, and page margins.
  • Reading Progress: Exact scroll position is remembered automatically.
  • Completion Detection: Scrolling past ~95% prompts a gentle “Looks like you finished this” banner to archive in one tap.
  • In-App Safari Reader Fallback: If a complex paywall or script interferes, tap the Safari icon for system web view.

Videos & Social Feeds (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X)

Clutch displays high-resolution poster art and cleanly hands off playback to the native host application (e.g. YouTube app) or Safari. We intentionally do not embed clunky web players that trigger playback error 152.

Text Highlights & Private Notes

  • Highlights: Select any text inside the reader and tap Highlight. Highlights are stored locally on that bookmark.
  • Private Bookmark Note: Scroll to the bottom of the article to jot down private thoughts (e.g. “Send this to Marcus”). Notes autosave as you type.

Extract Core Info (On-Device AI)

Tap the sparkle extraction icon in the reader toolbar to parse unstructured articles into structured data:

Content Type Extracted Output
🍳 Recipes Prep / cook / total times, yields, categorized ingredients checklist, numbered steps.
📖 Tutorials Required tools/materials, time estimates, ordered actionable instructions.
📝 Articles & News 3 to 5 grounded executive summary bullets with zero hallucinated fluff.
06

Organize: Tags & Collections

💡
Core Rule:
Tags classify (“What is this?” — e.g. Tech, Recipe, Finance).
Collections curate (“Where do I want this?” — e.g. Trip to Tokyo, Weekend Projects).

The Organize Sheet

Swipe left on any card or select Organize from the long-press menu:

  • Tags Tab: Toggle automatic AI categories or add custom user labels.
  • Collections Tab: Assign the item to one or more shelves, or create a New Collection with a custom SF Symbol.

Sub-Collections & Covers

  • Shelves support one level of nesting (sub-collections) to keep navigation tidy.
  • Set a custom cover photo or let Clutch automatically generate a mosaic cover from member bookmarks.
  • Non-Destructive Deletion: Deleting a collection shelf never deletes your bookmarks — only the organization group.
07

Library Segments

Navigate to the Library tab to browse your content across three dedicated views:

  • Shelves: View your custom curated collections with bookmark counts and cover art.
  • Types: Automatic format classification filters (Articles, Videos, Social Feeds, Forums, Documents).
  • Tags: Direct index to browse bookmarks by specific tags.

Suggest a Shelf (Apple Intelligence)

When you accumulate related unread bookmarks, a sparkle affordance appears in the Library. Tapping it generates a one-shot collection proposal with an AI-suggested title, symbol, and member items. Confirm with one tap or edit as you see fit.

08

Search & Filters

Natural Language Search

Search using natural conversational phrases:

  • "cookie recipes with butter"
  • "youtube videos saved last week"
  • "unread machine learning articles"

On devices with Apple Intelligence, queries compile into structured database predicates. On other devices, high-speed substring matching and tag indexing provide instant fallbacks.

Facet Filter Chips

Horizontal filter chips allow you to quickly narrow your view by Unread status, Content Types, or Tags. An active "Filtered" pill displays active criteria with a quick Clear button.

09

Up Next & Reminders

Up Next (Pinned Reads)

Pin up to 3 priority items to the top of your inbox. Long press any card and tap Pin to Up Next. Pinned items stay anchored above your stream until unpinned or archived.

Local Reminders

Long press an item, tap Remind Me, and select a time (e.g. Tonight, Tomorrow morning, or Weekend). Clutch schedules a native iOS notification locally on your device. Tapping the reminder deep-links directly into the reader.

Multi-Select Mode

Long press and tap Select to enter batch mode. Tap cards to select them, then use the bottom action bar to batch-archive, delete, pin, or add to a collection shelf simultaneously.

10

Daily Digest (Spoken Audio)

Clutch transforms unread articles saved over the past 48 hours into a seamless, spoken morning briefing.

Starting Playback

  • Inside App: Tap the More (…) menu on Home and choose Play Daily Digest.
  • Control Center: Add the Clutch Play Digest control for instantaneous one-tap listening.
  • Siri / Shortcuts: Simply speak: “Hey Siri, play my Daily Digest.”

Audio Player Capabilities

  • Mini player docks at the bottom of the screen and expands into full player.
  • Chapter skip buttons to jump between story summaries.
  • Speed adjustment from 0.75× to 2.0× with pitch preservation.
  • Full integration with Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, Control Center, AirPods gestures, and Apple CarPlay.
  • Incoming phone calls automatically pause playback cleanly and resume afterward.

Apple Intelligence Architecture

Every artificial intelligence feature in Clutch executes 100% on-device using Apple’s system FoundationModels. No OpenAI API keys, no Anthropic servers, no tracking, and zero telemetry.

Feature With Apple Intelligence Fallback (Non-AI Mode)
Auto-Tagging Contextual semantic categorization Heuristic domain & metadata classification
Summaries Structured At a Glance bullets Smart clipped first-paragraph extraction
Extract Core Info Recipe / Tutorial / Takeaways sheets Control hidden; clean reader active
Smart Search Natural language predicate interpretation Substring keyword search & tag filters
Suggest a Shelf Semantic grouping of related unread items Control hidden; manual shelves active
🛡️

Widgets, Siri & Spotlight

Home Screen & Lock Screen Widgets

Add Clutch widgets in Small, Medium, or Large sizes:

  • Small: Displays active unread count and your top Up Next pinned item.
  • Medium: Displays your top 3 unread stories with direct tap-to-read links.
  • Large: Includes pinned reads, upcoming scheduled reminders, and a 1-tap Play Digest button.

Spotlight Indexing

Saved bookmarks are indexed directly into iOS CoreSpotlight. Search for an article from your iPhone home screen to deep-link directly into Clutch’s reader.

Siri & App Shortcuts

  • “Save to Clutch” — Saves the active URL or clipboard text.
  • “Play my Daily Digest” — Begins continuous audio briefing immediately.
13

Inbox Zero & Trash

Archive Mechanics

Archiving represents “I’ve enjoyed and consumed this.” Swipe right on any card or tap Archive in the reader. Archived items move out of your stream into the Archive view.

30-Day Trash Safety Net

Deleting an item sends it to the Trash for 30 days. You can restore items at any point before the automatic purge. Nothing is permanently erased in fewer than two deliberate actions.

Optional Auto-Archive

If you prefer automated queue hygiene, enable auto-archive in Settings (options for 7, 14, or 30 days). This setting is off by default to respect user control.

Settings & Privacy

Sovereign Privacy Architecture

  • Zero Accounts: You never create an account, password, or profile.
  • Zero Servers: There is no Clutch backend receiving your bookmark database.
  • Zero Telemetry: No analytics trackers, ad SDKs, or third-party behavioral profiling.

16

Tips & Troubleshooting

Pro Tips

  • Safari First: Share from Safari when possible for the richest immediate thumbnail and clean article DOM.
  • Pin 3 Reads: Keep your top 3 reads in Up Next so you always know what to read next.
  • Audio Commutes: Listen to the Daily Digest on morning dog walks or commutes.
  • Offline Languages: In Settings, download offline speech models (~99 MB English or ~401 MB Multilingual/Arabic) for Natural Voice synthesis without internet.

Card Stuck on "Enriching"?

  1. Open the main app (enrichment executes in the app container, not the Share Sheet).
  2. Tap Tap to retry on the card or long press and select Retry Enrichment.
  3. Check your network connection. Links shared in Airplane Mode queue up and process as soon as connectivity resumes.

Share Sheet Missing Clutch?

Tap Share in Safari > scroll right on the app icons > tap More > tap Edit > enable Clutch and add it to Favorites.

Widget Appears Stale?

Open the app once to trigger an immediate App Group database reload, or remove and re-add the widget on your Home Screen.
