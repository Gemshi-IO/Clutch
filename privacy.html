Privacy Policy

Clutch (“the App”)
•
Last updated: November 11, 2026

Clutch (“the App”) is built to keep your reading private. This policy describes how the App handles information.

Summary:
We do not require an account. We do not operate a Clutch server that receives your bookmarks. We do not sell personal data. We do not send your bookmarks, documents, or reading content to any third-party AI service. On-device Apple Intelligence features process content locally when available.

Information on your device

Bookmarks (URLs, titles, article text, thumbnails, notes, tags, collections, reminders metadata, and related enrichment) are stored on your device using Apple’s frameworks (including SwiftData). Optionally, if you enable iCloud for this App, Apple may sync that library through your personal iCloud account under Apple’s terms — not through a Clutch backend.

Share Extension & widgets

The Share Extension and widgets read and write bookmark data only through the App Group container on your device so saves stay fast and local.

Natural Voice (on-device speech)

Premium English Natural Voice ships inside the App (no English model download). Optional Offline Languages downloads a separate multilingual speech model (~401 MB) for Arabic and other supported languages — only when you choose Download in Settings or when you Listen to a non-English story. Synthesis runs on device. Model downloads may come from public hosting such as Hugging Face; those requests download model files only and do not upload your bookmark text, documents, or other personal content. Your content is not sent to Clutch or to a third-party speech/AI API for narration.

Purchases

Unlimited unlock uses Apple’s StoreKit / In-App Purchase. Payment details are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment card information.

Apple Intelligence

When Apple Intelligence is available and you leave AI features enabled, the App may process bookmark or document text with Apple’s on-device Foundation Models for tagging, summaries, extraction, search interpretation, or shelf suggestions. That processing stays on your device. It is not uploaded to Clutch or to any third-party AI provider. If Apple Intelligence is unavailable or you turn those settings off, the App falls back to heuristics and keyword search.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We do not send push notifications from a Clutch server.

Analytics & tracking

The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites for advertising.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it to store sensitive information about children.

Your choices

You can delete bookmarks, empty Trash, clear cached text and thumbnails, export JSON, restore or manage purchases in Settings, and control notification permission in iOS Settings. Uninstalling the App removes on-device data for that install (iCloud copies follow your iCloud settings).

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
