# PocketBookmark — User Guide

**Save anything from anywhere in under a second — and never feel guilty about the pile again.**

PocketBookmark is a private, on-device read-later app for iPhone and iPad. Capture links and notes from Safari, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X, Reddit, and more. Organize them into shelves, listen to a Daily Digest, and extract recipes, tutorials, or key takeaways — all without an account or PocketBookmark server.

Your library syncs across your Apple devices with **iCloud**. Everything else stays on your device.

## 1. Getting started

### What you need

- iPhone or iPad on a recent iOS / iPadOS release that supports the app
- Optional but recommended: **iCloud** signed in (for sync across devices)
- Optional: **Apple Intelligence** enabled (for smart tags, Extract Core Info, Suggest a Shelf, smarter search, richer summaries)

### First launch

On first open you’ll see a short onboarding:

1. Welcome  
2. How to save with the Share Sheet  
3. Library overview  
4. Daily Digest  
5. Get Started  

You can skip anytime. The app is ready to use immediately after.

## 2. Saving links

PocketBookmark never drops a save. If the network is down or parsing fails, the URL still lands in your inbox and enrichment can retry later.

### Save from another app (fastest)

1. Open the page, video, or post you want to keep.  
2. Tap **Share**.  
3. Choose **PocketBookmark**.  
4. Confirm — you’ll see a quick checkmark.

Works from Safari, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Reddit, Notes, and most apps that share a URL.

**Safari tip:** Sharing from Safari often includes a better title and preview image right away. Sharing from social apps may start with a bare URL; PocketBookmark fills in the card when you open the main app.

### Save inside the app

1. On Home, tap **+**.  
2. Choose **Add Link**.  
3. Paste or type a URL (optional title).  
4. Optionally pick tags or a collection.  
5. Tap **Save**.

### Clipboard offer

When you return to PocketBookmark and there’s a link on the clipboard, you may see a gentle **“Link on clipboard?”** banner. Tap it to open the Add Link sheet — the app never silently reads your clipboard in the background.

### What happens after you save

Cards appear almost immediately (title + thumbnail when available), then enrich in the background:

- Cleaner title and description  
- Offline article text (when possible)  
- Thumbnail and soft color tint  
- Auto-tags (if Apple Intelligence is available; otherwise sensible defaults)

A shimmer means “still enriching.” **Tap to retry** appears if something failed — your bookmark is still there.

### Duplicates

Saving the same URL again bumps it to the top of your inbox and shows that it was already saved. Nothing is duplicated.

## 3. Saving notes

Notes are first-class items — not just comments on a link.

### Create a note

1. On Home, tap **+**.  
2. Choose **Add Note**.  
3. Write your text. Optionally add up to **5 photos**.  
4. Use **Bold** / **Italic** while editing.  
5. Tap **Done**.

### Share text as a note

Share plain text from Notes or other apps into PocketBookmark. If the text contains a URL, it’s saved as a **link**; otherwise it’s saved as a **Note**.

### Open and edit notes

- Tap a note card to open the reader.  
- Tap the **pencil** to edit.  
- In the reader you can toggle checklist items if you used checklist-style lines.  
- Attachments appear as a photo gallery.

## 4. Your inbox (Home)

Home is your living pile — not a filing cabinet.

### Layout

- **Grid (masonry)** — cards sized by their real aspect ratios (tall TikToks next to wide articles).  
- **List** — denser rows with thumbnail + title.

Toggle layout from the Home action bar (grid ⇄ list).

### Card gestures

| Gesture | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Tap** | Open reader / player |
| **Swipe right** | Archive (mark as consumed) |
| **Swipe left** | Organize (tags + collections) |
| **Long press** | Context menu (all actions) |

Swipes are accelerators — everything is also available from the long-press menu and accessibility actions.

### Useful context menu actions

- Show More (peek full title, description, tags, notes, reminder)  
- Add to Collection / Organize  
- Pin to Up Next  
- Remind Me  
- Copy Link / Share  
- Edit Thumbnail / Retry Enrichment (when available)  
- Edit Note (notes)  
- Delete  
- Select (enter multi-select)

### Aging section

Items older than **14 days** fade slightly and move under **Aging**. You may see a dismissible **Tidy Up** prompt to archive the whole aging set in one tap. Automatic archive-after-N-days is optional in Settings (off by default).

### Archive vs Inbox

Use the tray / archive control on Home to switch between your active inbox and archived items.

## 5. Reading & watching

Tap any card to open it with a smooth zoom transition.

### Articles & Reddit posts

- Distraction-free reader with adjustable **font, size, spacing, margins,** and **theme** (System / Sepia / Paper / Black)  
- Optional **At a glance** summary when it truly compresses the article  
- Scroll progress is remembered; reopen to resume near where you left off  
- Near the end (~95%), you may see **“Looks like you finished this”** → Archive  
- **Open in Reader Mode** — in-app Safari Reader when local text is thin or missing  
- **Open in Safari** / Share always available for http(s) links  

### Video & social (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X)

PocketBookmark shows a **poster image** and opens the content in the **host app or Safari**. Playback isn’t embedded inside the reader (this keeps playback reliable).

### Listen in the reader

On text-heavy items, use the headphones control:

- **Read Summary** — spoken summary (when available)  
- **Read Full** — reads the article / caption text  

Only one narrator runs at a time (reader speech and Daily Digest won’t talk over each other).

### Private notes on a bookmark

Below the article metadata you can add a short private note (“try this sauce”, “send to Sam”). It autosaves as you type.

### Text highlights

On article body (and caption blocks), select text → **Highlight**. Highlights are saved on that bookmark. Remove highlight from the selection menu.

### Extract Core Info

When Apple Intelligence is available, tap the Extract control in the reader toolbar (icon adapts: frying pan for recipes, book for tutorials, list for takeaways).

You’ll get a structured sheet:

| Type | What you get |
|------|----------------|
| **Recipe** | Prep / cook / yield, ingredients, steps |
| **Tutorial** | Time, materials, ordered steps |
| **Key takeaways** | 3–5 grounded bullets |

Share or copy the whole extraction. **Retry** regenerates. Results are cached on the bookmark.

## 6. Organize: tags & collections

**Tags classify** (“what is this?”).  
**Collections curate** (“where do I want this?”).

### Organize sheet

Open it by:

- Swiping **left** on a card, or  
- Long press → **Organize** / **Add to Collection…**

One sheet, two jobs:

1. **Tags** — toggle AI taxonomy tags and your own labels  
2. **Collections** — toggle shelves; create **New Collection…** (name + SF Symbol)

Collection membership is drafted while the sheet is open and applied when you tap **Done**.

### Custom tags

You can create and edit your own tags in Organize. AI tags stay a closed vocabulary so filters don’t sprawl into synonym soup.

### Sub-collections & covers

- Shelves can have **one level** of sub-collections.  
- Long-press a shelf → **Add Sub-Collection** or **Edit Cover**.  
- Covers can be a photo you pick, or an auto mosaic from members.  
- Deleting a collection **never** deletes bookmarks — only the shelf.

## 7. Library

Open **Library** from Home (collections / shelves destination).

Three segments:

| Segment | What it shows |
|---------|----------------|
| **Shelves** | Your collections (top-level), with counts and covers |
| **Types** | Auto filters: Article, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Reddit, Notes, etc. |
| **Tags** | Browse by tag |

### Inside a shelf

- Grid or list layout (independent from Home’s layout preference)  
- Toolbar: **+** (add link into this shelf), Sub-Collection (top-level only), Settings  
- Multi-select to archive, remove from collection, or move to another shelf  

### Suggest a Shelf

When Apple Intelligence is on and you have enough unread items, Library → Shelves may show a **sparkles** control.

1. Tap it.  
2. Review the suggested name, icon, and members.  
3. Edit if you like.  
4. Confirm **Create**.

One-shot suggestion only — no chatbot.

## 8. Search & filters

### Search on Home (and in Library browse)

Type naturally:

- Keywords (“pasta”)  
- Platform vibes (“youtube”, “videos”)  
- Time (“last week”)  
- Unread / archived  

With Apple Intelligence, smart search turns your phrase into structured filters. Without it, plain text + tag search still works.

### Facet chips

Above the grid you’ll see chips for unread, platforms, tags, and archived (depending on context). When filters are active, a **Filtered: …** summary and **Clear** appear.

## 9. Up Next, reminders & select mode

### Up Next (pin)

Pin up to **3** items to keep them at the top of Home.

- Long press → **Pin**, or use Select mode  
- Unpin from the Up Next strip or the menu  

### Remind Me

Long press → **Remind Me** → pick a time. You’ll get a local notification. Tapping it can deep-link back into that item.

### Select mode

1. Long press → **Select**, or use the More (**…**) menu → Select.  
2. Tap cards to toggle selection.  
3. Use **Select All** / **Deselect All** on the banner (visible items only).  
4. Bulk archive, delete, add to collection, or pin.  

Deselecting all does **not** exit select mode — tap **Done** when finished.

## 10. Daily Digest (audio)

Turn unread articles from about the **last 48 hours** into a personal spoken briefing.

### Play a digest

- Home → More (**…**) → **Play Daily Digest**, or  
- Control Center **Play Digest** control, or  
- Siri / Shortcuts: “Play my Daily Digest”

### While it plays

- Mini player on Home expands into the full Digest player  
- Skip forward / back between stories  
- Change speed (about 0.75×–2×)  
- Lock screen, Control Center, AirPods, and CarPlay controls work  
- A phone call pauses playback instead of killing it  

If Apple Intelligence isn’t available, the digest still reads clipped article text.

## 11. Apple Intelligence features

All AI runs **on-device**. Features hide themselves (they don’t sit disabled and confusing) when the model isn’t available.

| Feature | What it does | Without AI |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| Auto-tags | Suggests tags from a fixed list | Domain / heuristic tags |
| Summaries | Short “at a glance” + digest scripts | Clipped text |
| Extract Core Info | Recipe / tutorial / takeaways sheet | Button hidden |
| Smart search | Natural language → filters | Substring + tags |
| Suggest a Shelf | One-shot collection proposal | Control hidden |

Turn individual AI preferences on or off in **Settings** when you want heuristics only.

## 12. Widgets, Siri & Spotlight

### Home Screen widget

Add the **PocketBookmark** widget (Small / Medium / Large):

- Unread count  
- **Up next** (pinned first, then newest unread)  
- **Coming up** reminders (next 7 days) on larger sizes

Add the **Play Digest** control to start a digest without opening the app first.

### Siri & Shortcuts

Useful intents include:

- Save to PocketBookmark  
- Play my Daily Digest  

### Spotlight

Saved items are indexed for system search. Find a bookmark from the Home Screen search field without opening the app.

## 13. Inbox Zero & Trash

### Archive

Archive means “I’ve consumed this” — swipe right, reader Archive button, or the end-of-article prompt. Archived items live in the Archive view; they still count toward free-tier active slots until deleted.

### Delete & Trash

Delete sends items to **Trash** for **30 days**, then they’re purged. Restore anytime from Trash before then.

### Auto-archive (optional)

In Settings you can enable automatic archive after 7 / 14 / 30 days. Default is **off**. Nothing is permanently deleted without Trash.

## 14. Free plan & Unlimited

### Free

- **20 active items** (not deleted; archive still counts toward the 20)  
- Saves **never fail** over the cap — you can still capture; enrichment for overflow may pause until you free slots or unlock  
- **50 unique bookmarks** across collections (first-time joins are gated; removing always works)

### Unlimited (one-time unlock)

Purchase **PocketBookmark Unlimited** (non-consumable) to remove the active-item and collection-member caps. Restore purchases on a new device from Settings.

## 15. Settings & privacy

### Appearance

App-wide light / dark / system. Reader has its own paper themes for long-form reading.

### iCloud sync

When you’re signed into iCloud, Settings shows sync status. Your library, including Share Extension saves and widgets, uses the same iCloud-backed store. There is **no** PocketBookmark account.

### Privacy stance

- No PocketBookmark servers  
- No tracking analytics product  
- No third-party LLM APIs  
- On-device Apple Intelligence only  

See in-app **Privacy**, **Terms**, and **Acknowledgements** for details.

### Support & About

Version / build, rate the app, email support, and legal pages live under Settings.

## 16. Tips & troubleshooting

### Quick tips

- Share from **Safari** when you want the richest first paint.  
- Use **collections** for projects (“Trip”, “Recipes”) and **tags** for type (“Tech”, “Video”).  
- Pin your next three reads to **Up Next** so the pile feels intentional.  
- Play **Daily Digest** on a commute — skip stories you don’t need.  
- Use **Extract Core Info** on recipes and how-tos; on news you’ll usually get key takeaways.

### Card stuck enriching or failed

1. Open the app (enrichment runs in the main app, not in the Share Sheet).  
2. Tap **Tap to retry** on the card, or long press → **Retry Enrichment**.  
3. Check network. Airplane-mode shares queue and drain when you’re back online.

### Share Sheet doesn’t show PocketBookmark

Edit the Share Sheet list → enable PocketBookmark. Confirm the Share Extension is installed with the app.

### AI features missing

Open Settings → Apple Intelligence / device eligibility. On unsupported devices, core save / read / organize still work; AI controls simply stay hidden.

### Widget looks stale

Open the app once (sync + reload), or remove and re-add the widget. Widget data comes from the shared App Group store.

### Sync

Sign into iCloud on each device. Give CloudKit a moment after first launch or a big import.
