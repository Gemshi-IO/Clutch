The Universal Canvas

Your library. Your rules.

The saved content is the product — our UI is a frame, never a painting. Browse an asymmetrical masonry grid sized by true aspect ratios, or collapse into a dense high-volume list.

01

Content-Derived Liquid Glass

There is no fixed brand color. Dominant color is extracted once at ingest using Core Image CIKMeans and subtly tints backgrounds, glass borders, and reader chrome at an ultra-fine 18% opacity.

02

Zero-Friction Ingestion Engine

Save from Safari, X, TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, or YouTube under 120MB memory ceilings. Saves write immediately to a shared SwiftData store in under a second — enrichment happens quietly in the background.

03

Gestures Over Buttons

Swipe right to archive. Swipe left to organize into collections. Long press for instant actions: Listen to Summary, Extract Core Info, Copy Link, and Share.

04

60fps Shortest-Column Layout

Custom shortest-column LazyVStack masonry avoids eager layout measuring, sustaining 60 frames per second past thousands of saved items.

The Audio Engine

Your morning commute podcast.
Generated from your saves.

"Play Daily Digest" gathers unread articles from the last 48 hours, map-reduces them into a cohesive spoken script with the on-device model, and narrates them aloud.

Segmented Chapter Narration

Scripts are segmented per article, not a single opaque audio blob. Skip forward or back between stories, view full lock-screen Now Playing metadata, and tap through straight to the source article in the reader.

✓
Lock Screen & Dynamic Island Ready

Personal Voice Opt-In

Inbox Zero Mechanics

Aging, not deleting.

Nobody trusts an app that deletes their stuff. Clutch drains your queue through natural reading habits and gentle lifecycle management — zero guilt streaks.

⏳

14-Day Gentle Aging

Items older than 14 days fade slightly and group into an "Aging" section. A periodic, dismissible "Tidy Up" affordance offers one-tap batch archive with full undo support.

📖

Auto-Archive on Completion

Scrolling past 95% of an article or finishing a video prompts to archive it inline. Your queue naturally drains as you enjoy content, without feeling like homework.

🛡️

30-Day Trash Safety Net

Nothing is ever permanently deleted in fewer than two deliberate steps. Deleted items rest safely in Trash for 30 days before automatic purge.

Yes! There is zero limit on saves in the free version — you can save any number of links, videos, and articles directly into your inbox without paying.

On the free tier, your first 20 saves receive full background AI enrichment (metadata, previews, tags, and reader mode), and you can organize up to 50 bookmarks across your collection shelves. To unlock unlimited background enrichment for all saves and unlimited collection capacity, we offer a single StoreKit 2 non-consumable lifetime unlock (pocketbookmark.unlimited) — no recurring subscriptions.
Clutch adheres strictly to our principle: "Degrade gracefully, never blank." Every single feature has an offline heuristic fallback. If Apple Intelligence is unavailable, auto-tagging uses domain heuristics, search uses substring ranking, and the Daily Digest reads clipped article text with Apple's premium TTS.
Unlike clunky in-reader web embeds that trigger playback errors, Clutch grabs the high-resolution poster thumbnail and cleanly opens the video inside its native host application or Safari. Your inbox stays fast, lightweight, and clutter-free.
Yes, cross-device iCloud sync is fully supported. Powered by SwiftData and Apple's CloudKit, your saved bookmarks, read states, AI tags, and collection shelves automatically synchronize seamlessly across all your iPhones and iPads under your personal private iCloud account. No third-party accounts or external servers are involved.
